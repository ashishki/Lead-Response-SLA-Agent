# Draft Pilot Terms

Date: 2026-05-20
Vertical: DFW emergency garage door repair
Status: non-legal commercial draft for buyer discussion

Pre-pilot note: the public demo report and first-10 target list support only a
manual replay/pilot conversation. They are not paid terms and do not prove
conversion lift, ROI, autonomous-send safety, or paid production readiness.
Before accepting real lead data or payment, a human must approve privacy/legal
wording, retention promises, and subprocessor commitments.

## Pilot Offer

Run a 14-day lead-response pilot for one garage door repair company using website forms and one messaging or callback channel.

The system will:

- acknowledge new leads quickly
- collect required qualification fields
- answer only from approved knowledge
- route pricing commitments, safety advice, complaints, high-value commercial leads, and uncertain bookings to human review
- produce a weekly buyer report

## Commercial Terms To Test

Preferred first ask: Recovery Pilot package.

- Setup: $500
- Pilot: $300/week for two weeks
- Renewal option: choose fixed monthly, booked-lead share, or dispatcher-assist package after the pilot report

Alternative if buyer resists upfront payment:

- No setup fee
- $150 per incremental booked job attributed to the pilot
- Cap: $1,500 for the first month
- Buyer must label booked/lost/no-response outcomes weekly

## Buyer Responsibilities

- Provide approved FAQ, service area, pricing-range, booking, and escalation text.
- Confirm baseline and pilot measurement dates.
- Label outcomes weekly: booked, qualified handoff, lost, no response, disqualified.
- Assign one operator for human-review approvals.
- Avoid pasting raw customer PII into report notes or feedback docs.

## Provider Responsibilities

- Configure one inbound source and one outbound channel for the pilot.
- Maintain approved knowledge and unsafe/handoff policy.
- Provide weekly report with response time, lead capture, booked calls/jobs, qualified handoffs, review rate, cost per lead, provider failures, and unsafe automation count.
- Keep unsupported, unsafe, or uncertain cases in human review.

## Success Criteria

The pilot is commercially successful if at least one is true:

- at least 3 incremental booked jobs are recovered in 14 days
- booked-job rate improves by at least 10 percent relative to baseline
- median first response drops under 2 minutes with zero unsafe autonomous replies
- buyer says qualified handoffs or reduced review workload justify continuing

## Stop Criteria

Stop or redesign if:

- the buyer cannot provide baseline data or outcome labels
- the agent creates any unsafe autonomous reply
- human-review rate remains above 50 percent after week one
- buyer cannot connect the report to revenue, saved labor, or response quality

## Objections To Capture

| Objection | What to learn |
|-----------|---------------|
| Too expensive | Ask whether fixed, performance, or hybrid pricing fits better. |
| Attribution unclear | Define what counts as an incremental booked job before launch. |
| Too much review work | Measure review minutes and identify missing knowledge/policy. |
| Safety concern | Show no-send and human-review rules; ask which categories must always be manual. |
| Integration concern | Start with one inbound source and one outbound channel. |

## Non-Legal Notes

This is a commercial discovery draft, not a legal contract. Before a paid pilot, convert terms into a short order form covering payment, data handling, approved channels, liability boundaries, cancellation, and support contact.
