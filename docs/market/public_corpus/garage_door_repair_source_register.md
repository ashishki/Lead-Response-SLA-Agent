## Demo Use Summary

- Service-area evidence supports DFW-area qualification, not firm service
  promises for a future tenant.
- Emergency-claim evidence supports urgent lead scenarios, not measured response
  or booking outcomes.
- Pricing-range evidence supports broad range language and exact-price handoff,
  not a final quote.
- Booking-rule evidence supports intake-field and confirmation scenarios, not
  autonomous booking.
- Safety evidence supports refusing DIY spring/cable instructions and routing
  to human review.
- Commercial evidence supports high-value routing for warehouse, dock, gate,
  roll-up, and multi-door cases.
