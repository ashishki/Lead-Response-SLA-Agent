# First 10 Manual Outreach Targets

Date: 2026-05-23
Vertical: DFW emergency/same-day garage door repair
Outreach owner: founder-led manual outreach only
Source protocol: `docs/market/open_source_research_protocol.md`
Demo pack: `docs/market/demo_report_garage_door_repair.md`

This list is for manually reviewed outreach. Do not execute automated email,
SMS, social, form-fill, robocall, scraping, enrichment, or product-agent
outreach. Use public business pages only to decide who might care about a
narrow replay/pilot conversation.

## Outreach Channel

Primary channel: call the public business number during business hours and ask
for the owner, operations manager, or dispatch manager.

Secondary channel: submit a short public website contact form only when the
site is clearly meant for business inquiries. Do not paste automated sequences
or send bulk messages.

Ask for a narrow conversation:

"I built a public-source garage-door replay demo using synthetic leads, not your
customer data. Could I show you the 10-minute replay and ask whether your real
lead-response workflow has the same missed-call, after-hours, or busy-dispatch
problem?"

## Conversation Plan

1. Confirm this is manual research, not automated outreach.
2. Ask whether the owner or dispatcher handles urgent leads during busy,
   after-hours, or technician-on-job windows.
3. Show the public demo report only if they want context.
4. Ask whether a 10-minute replay against synthetic garage-door scenarios is
   useful enough to compare against their real workflow.
5. If interested, ask for a narrow pilot-readiness conversation, not broad data
   access or production deployment.

## Proof Questions

Ask only about past behavior and pilot feasibility:

1. In the last 30 days, about how many inbound calls or forms did not receive a
   same-hour response?
2. How are after-hours emergency leads handled today?
3. Which lead sources matter most: phone, website forms, Google Business
   Profile, Local Services Ads, referrals, or another source?
4. What would make an automated first reply unacceptable or unsafe?
5. Would a two-week replay/pilot be useful if it starts with human approval on
   every proposed reply?

## Outreach Boundaries

- No automated outreach is executed by the product or agent.
- Do not claim conversion lift, ROI, autonomous-send safety, or paid production
  readiness.
- Do not ask for private lead logs on the first touch.
- If a buyer volunteers real lead data, pause and require explicit approval for
  data handling, privacy, retention, and use in evaluation before accepting it.
- Keep notes at the account/process level; do not record customer names, phone
  numbers, emails, addresses, transcript text, or appointment details.

## Validation Gate

Continue to a replay/pilot conversation only if at least 5 of these 10 accounts
confirm urgent lead-response pain, and at least 3 agree that a narrow
human-approved replay or two-week pilot would be worth reviewing.

Fail or pivot if the common answer is "we already answer every lead quickly,"
"our answering service books reliably," or "slow response is not causing lost
jobs."
