# First 10 Target Accounts

Date: 2026-05-20
Vertical: DFW emergency/same-day garage door repair
Outreach owner: founder-led manual outreach

## Outreach Channel

Primary channel: call the public business number during business hours and ask for the owner, operations manager, or dispatch manager.

Secondary channel: website contact form with a short note about missed after-hours/busy-hour leads and a request for a 15-minute call-log review.

Do not pitch generic AI. Ask for recent past behavior: missed calls, stale web forms, after-hours voicemail, and jobs lost because another company responded first.

## Qualification Questions

Ask only about past behavior:

1. In the last 30 days, about how many inbound calls or forms did not get a response within one hour?
2. How many after-hours calls reached voicemail, and how many became booked jobs?
3. What does one booked repair job usually gross for spring, opener, cable, or off-track issues?
4. Who handles dispatch when the owner or dispatcher is unavailable?
5. What would make an automated first reply unacceptable or unsafe?

## Validation Gate

Proceed to vertical policy pack only if at least 5 of these 10 accounts confirm urgent lead-response pain and at least 3 agree to share anonymized response-time or missed-lead data for a two-week pilot.

Fail or pivot if the common answer is "we already answer every lead quickly," "our answering service books reliably," or "slow response is not causing lost jobs."
